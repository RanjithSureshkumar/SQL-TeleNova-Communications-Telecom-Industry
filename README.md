# SQL-Tele-Communications-Telecom-Industry
# Types of Analysis Performed
# A. Customer & Subscriber Analysis
•	Active customers by type – Prepaid vs Postpaid, total customer counts.

•	Customer distribution – By city tier (Tier 1/2/3) and urban/rural flag.

•	Subscriber network usage – 4G only, 5G only, or both.
 
# B. Usage & Consumption Analysis
•	Total data consumption per customer – Identify high data users and patterns.

•	Data usage by plan category – Compare Heavy, Medium, Light plan usage.

•	Plan data utilization ratio – Actual data used vs allowed to find underutilized plans.

 
# C. Revenue & ARPU Analysis
•	ARPU by telecom circle – Which circles generate the most revenue per user.

•	ARPU by customer type – Prepaid vs Postpaid revenue efficiency.

•	Plan revenue contribution – Revenue generated by each plan.

•	High-volume but low ARPU plans – Plans with many subscribers but low revenue per user.
 
# D. Churn & Retention Analysis
•	Overall churn rate – Company-wide churn percentage.

•	Churn by circle – High-risk telecom circles.

•	Top churn reasons – Price, service, competitors.

•	Churn by competitor – Which competitor gains the most churned customers.

•	High-revenue, low-usage customer segments – Retention targets.
 
# E. Revenue Leakage & Operational Sustainability
•	Revenue leakage by customer type – Discounts + refunds causing revenue loss.

•	Operationally unsustainable plans – High usage but low revenue, costing more than earning.
